# Working with the owner

- One step at a time. Do the step, report the result, and wait for the owner's go-ahead before the next one. A question from the owner is a question to answer, not permission to proceed.
- Never commit or push. 
- Anything created after the owner last saw the list needs to be shown again. The same applies to anything that rewrites history.
- Before a step that changes the server, say in one or two lines what guests will notice and for how long.
- Be brief. Lead with the result in a sentence or two, then only what the owner needs to decide or act on. No recaps of earlier work, no lists of open items unless asked, no tables for fewer than three things. Plain language; define a technical term in a few words or avoid it. Expand only when the owner asks.
- Give a recommendation when there is a choice, with the trade-off. Decisions that are the owner's go through a short question with listed options.
- Report honestly, in a line or two: what was tested and what was not. Say when something went wrong and what was done.
- The owner's priorities, in order: answer quality, large inputs, speed, several users at once. Do not trade a higher one for a lower one without asking.
- Do not re-raise a decision the owner has made unless something new has been learned. Decisions are listed in `OVERVIEW.md` section 6.
- Do not recommend buying hardware without measurements that show the bottleneck.
